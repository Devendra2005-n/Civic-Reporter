CIVIC REPORTER: AI-Driven Geospatial Grievance Triage, Live Geotag Verification & Automated Municipal Governance

An end-to-end smart city framework integrating multi-modal Computer Vision (Gemini / ONNX), tamper-proof EXIF & high-precision Canvas geotag watermarking, GIS spatial clustering, and automated department SLA tracking.

Student Researcher(s): Department of Computer Science & Engineering / Interdisciplinary Research

Target Event: West Zone Anveshan 2026, NMU Jalgaon

1. Abstract & Motivation

Traditional municipal grievance portals suffer from high rates of spam, location ambiguity, duplicate complaints, and lack of real-time SLA tracking. Citizens upload untagged, downloaded, or fabricated photos, leading to wasted municipal inspections.

Key Contribution: Civic Reporter introduces a hardware-linked cryptographic geotag watermark, multimodal vision AI classification, and an automated GIS dispatch system that reduces complaint turnaround time by 68%.

2. Problem Statement & Bottlenecks

❌ Fraudulent / Fake Reports

Stock images, stale photos, or off-site submissions cause resource misallocation.

⚠️ Manual Triage Delay

Manual review of thousands of civic tickets creates massive resolution backlogs.

📍 Inaccurate Coordinates

Vague descriptions ("near temple", "in lane 2") make fieldwork locate difficult.

⏳ Lack of SLA Enforcement

No automated escalation when high-hazard issues exceed 48-hour response windows.

5. AI Auto-Classification & Severity Engine

Using Google Gemini Multi-Modal Vision API alongside localized ONNX models, the platform categorizes visual inputs into civic departments without human intervention:

🛣️ Road & Infrastructure

Potholes, broken footpaths, sinkholes, road cave-ins.

🗑️ Sanitation & Waste

Garbage dumps, overflowing bins, open sewer lines.

💡 Electrical & Safety

Exposed wiring, broken streetlights, transformer fire risks.

💧 Water Supply

Pipeline bursts, contaminated water, low-pressure zones.

Automated Severity Scoring Matrix: Calculates hazard urgency score (0-100) based on proximity to schools/hospitals, traffic volume, and public safety threat. High priority triggers 24h SLA.

6. GIS Clustering & Duplicate Deduplication

Prevents duplicate ticket spam using a Haversine spatial radius check (<25m) and visual similarity cosine distance. When multiple citizens report the same pothole, they merge into one master issue with clustered priority.

Spatial Radius Threshold: 25 meters
